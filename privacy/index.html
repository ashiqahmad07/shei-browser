Shei Browser

Privacy policy

Last updated 20 October 2026

Shei Browser is an Android web browser made by Ashiq Ahmad. It has no accounts, no analytics and no ads of its own. What you do in the browser stays on your phone, apart from the requests described below that the browser needs in order to work. We never receive your browsing history, your searches or your files.

Kept on your phone

Your history, bookmarks, open tabs, downloads, settings, saved passwords, and the data websites save (cookies, cached pages and site storage) are stored only on your phone. Saved passwords are encrypted with a key held by Android's keystore, and are shown only after you confirm your phone's screen lock. Pages you open in private tabs are not added to your history.

To delete this data, use Settings › Clear browsing data, delete items from History, Bookmarks, Downloads or Passwords, or uninstall the app.

What leaves your phone

Websites you visit

Like any browser, Shei Browser sends each website your IP address, the kind of device and browser you use, and any cookies that site set earlier. What a website does with this is covered by that website's own policy. Unless you switch it off in Settings, the browser also asks each website not to sell or share your data (Global Privacy Control).

Search and suggestions

When you search, your query goes to the search engine you chose in Settings (Google, Bing or DuckDuckGo). While you type in the address bar of a normal tab, what you have typed so far is sent to that engine to fetch suggestions. Nothing is sent for suggestions from a private tab, or when what you type is a web address.

Site icons

To show site icons on your shortcuts, tabs, history and bookmarks, the app sends each site's domain name (for example, example.com) to Google's icon service. This is not done for private tabs.

Ad-blocking lists

About once a day the app downloads updated ad and tracker lists from EasyList (easylist.to), AdGuard (filters.adtidy.org) and GitHub. These requests contain nothing about your browsing.

Browser engine updates

The browser engine (Mozilla GeckoView) downloads updated rules for rejecting cookie banners from Mozilla's servers. The first time you translate a page from or to a language, it downloads that language's translation model from the same servers. These requests contain nothing about the pages you visit, and pages are translated on your phone, never sent anywhere.

Protection from dangerous sites

The browser engine checks pages and downloads against Google Safe Browsing. It downloads lists of known dangerous sites to your phone. When an address matches an entry, it sends Google a short fragment of a hash of that address to confirm the match, not the address itself.

QR code scanner

The scanner uses the camera only while it is open. Images are read on your phone by Google ML Kit and are never sent anywhere. ML Kit may send Google information about your device and how the scanner performed, without any images.

Casting

When you cast a video, its address is sent to the Cast device you picked on your network, which then loads the video itself. The video player finds Cast devices with Google's Cast library, which sends Google information about finding devices and casting sessions that Google says is anonymous.

Proxy

If you set up a proxy under Settings › Proxy, your browsing goes through the proxy server you entered, and whoever runs that server can see it.

Permissions

Camera

Used only by the QR code scanner, and only after you allow it.

Videos on your phone

Not asked for. To play a video from your phone, you choose it in Android's own picker, and the app can open only the video you chose. It is not uploaded.

Notifications

Shows download progress and media playback controls.

Display over other apps

Lets a video keep playing in a floating window while you use other apps. It is asked for only when you first use the floating player.

What we don't do

We don't sell or share your information, show you ads, or track you across apps or websites. Shei Browser is not aimed at children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will appear here and in the app, with a new date.
